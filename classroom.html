The Problem

Regular YouTube Is a Gamble in a Classroom

Handing a student the YouTube app for one lesson video means accepting that the algorithm is also in the room — and it doesn't care about your subject.

Autoplay doesn't stop when the lesson does

A five-minute lesson clip becomes ten unrelated minutes the moment autoplay picks the next video. NoSuggest removes autoplay entirely — when a video ends, nothing starts on its own.

Shorts are one tap away from anywhere

The regular YouTube app keeps a Shorts tab sitting permanently next to every video. The moment attention drifts, it's there. In NoSuggest, the Shorts feed doesn't exist.

NoSuggest shows only what you added

No recommendations, no trending feed, no search bar leading elsewhere. Students see exactly the channels you set up — nothing more — on any phone, tablet, Chromebook, or laptop, with no sign-up required.

Before You Start — Important

Two Ways to Share a Feed. Know the Difference.

These two features look similar but do opposite things to whatever is already on a student's device. Using the wrong one mid-term can silently wipe saved videos.

What you're doing Export / Import Create a Pack
Effect on existing data Overwrites — replaces all channels and saved videos Appends only — adds channels, touches nothing else
What's transferred Channels and saved videos Channels only — no saved videos, nothing personal
If a channel already exists on the device Irrelevant — everything is replaced anyway Skipped automatically, no duplicates
Best used for First-time class setup at the start of a term Adding new channels mid-term without disturbing anything
How students receive it A text backup code, pasted into Import Data A tappable link — one tap adds all channels
Simple rule: use Export / Import at the start of the term. Use Create a Pack for anything you add after that — it will never wipe a student's saved videos or channels they added themselves.
Step-by-Step

Setting Up the Class Feed

The teacher does steps 1–3 once, on their own device. Students do step 4 — it takes under a minute.

Teacher
1
Add your subject's channels Open NoSuggest and search for every educational channel, documentary source, or creator you want the class to see.
2
Export a backup code Go to Settings → Export Data. NoSuggest generates a backup code and copies it to your clipboard.
3
Share it wherever students look Paste the code into Google Classroom, email, a class WhatsApp group — anywhere students already check.
4
Optional: lock it with Kids Mode Set a 6-digit PIN in Settings to hide the Search and Add Channel tabs — students can only watch from the approved list.
Student
1
Copy the code your teacher shared Copy the full backup code from Classroom, email, or chat.
2
Open nosuggest.com — no sign-up needed Works in any browser on any device. Go to Settings → Backup & Restore.
3
Tap Import Data → paste → Restore Tap the paste area, confirm it looks right, then tap Restore. Your feed now matches the class.
—
That's it — no account, no download, nothing from an app store.
Heads up for students: Import Data replaces all channels and saved videos already on your device. If you've saved your own videos and want to keep them, ask your teacher for a Create a Pack link instead — it only adds channels and leaves everything else untouched.
Mid-Term Updates

Adding New Channels Without Wiping Anything

Week 6 and you want to add three documentary channels that weren't in the original setup? Create a Pack is exactly what this is for.

1

Add just the new channels

On your device, add only the channels you want to introduce. Leave everything else alone.

2

Settings → Create a Pack

NoSuggest turns your current channel list into a single shareable link and copies it to your clipboard.

3

Share the link the usual way

Post it in Classroom or chat. Students tap the link, see which channels are in it, and tap once to add them.

4

Nothing else on their device changes

Channels they already have are skipped automatically. Saved videos are untouched. No surprises.

About the pack link: it carries channel names and IDs only — no saved videos, no personal data from your device. If adding would push a student past the 25-channel limit, NoSuggest adds as many as it can and reports how many were skipped.
