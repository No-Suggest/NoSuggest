What Is NoSuggest?

If you found this page from one of the listings below: NoSuggest is a free, privacy-first way to watch YouTube. You choose the channels, and it shows only their latest videos — no recommendations, no Shorts, no autoplay, no trending feed, no sign-in required. Videos still play in YouTube's own official player, so creators get full credit for every view. Nothing is downloaded, stored, or tracked, and NoSuggest is free forever with no premium tier.
