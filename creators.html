The Basics

What Is NoSuggest?

NoSuggest is a free, privacy-first way to watch YouTube. A viewer chooses which channels to follow, and NoSuggest shows only their latest videos — nothing recommended, nothing trending, no Shorts, no autoplay, no sign-in.

Every video, unchanged

Videos still play in YouTube's own official player. NoSuggest doesn't download, rehost, or alter anything — it only removes the clutter YouTube usually builds around a video.

A feed made only of channels a viewer chose

No sidebar of competing suggestions, no "Up Next" queue picking the next video — just the channels someone actually decided to follow.

Free, with nothing to sell

No ads of its own, no premium tier, no personal data collected. It runs on voluntary donations, which is also why I'm asking creators for help rather than paying for ads.

Why I'm Asking

Why I'm Reaching Out to Creators First

Doomscrolling and algorithm-driven "brain rot" viewing aren't just a parenting complaint anymore — plenty of creators, educators, and viewers themselves have raised the same concern: an endless Shorts feed and autoplay chain can turn a five-minute video into an hour nobody chose. NoSuggest removes the specific mechanics that cause that. I don't have an ad budget or a growth team — it's just me building and running this — so I'm asking the people who already have an audience's trust — creators — to consider sharing it, especially with anyone whose viewers include kids or teens.

No Shorts feed

The tab that turns "one quick video" into forty-five minutes of unrelated scrolling simply isn't part of NoSuggest.

No autoplay chain

When a video ends, nothing starts on its own. Watching stays a series of choices instead of something that happens passively.

A gentler default for younger viewers

Paired with NoSuggest's Kids Mode, a parent can hand over a device that only ever shows an approved list of channels — nothing more.

This is the part I'd ask you to share even if it did nothing for your own numbers. What comes next is honestly how it also happens to work in your favor — I just didn't want to lead with that.

What's different is what happens after a video ends. On YouTube, that's usually when autoplay or a Shorts scroll takes over and a viewer's attention drifts somewhere unrelated to what they came for. In NoSuggest, a viewer only sees the channels they've chosen to follow — so if someone follows you (and anyone you collaborate with) through NoSuggest, their next watch is more likely to actually be one of you, simply because that's what's there. It's less about holding attention and more about there being fewer places for it to accidentally go.

The Tool For This

Create a Pack: Share Your Channels in One Link

Create a Pack turns a list of channels into a single shareable link. For a creator, that list doesn't have to be just your own channel.

1

Just your channel

The simplest pack — one link that adds your channel to a fan's feed, so it's there every time they open NoSuggest.

2

Your channel plus collaborators

Add every channel you co-host, guest on, or regularly work with. One link introduces a fan to your whole circle at once — good for everyone in it.

3

Your channel plus creators you rate

Include channels you genuinely respect or want to point people toward — a recommendation list you curated, not one an algorithm assembled.

How it behaves: a pack only ever adds channels to a viewer's feed — it never removes or replaces what they already follow, and any channel they already have is simply skipped.
Step-by-Step

Building and Sharing Your Pack

1

Open NoSuggest and add channels

Search for and add your own channel, then any collaborators or related creators you want to include.

2

Go to Settings → Create a Pack

NoSuggest turns your current channel list into a single link, ready to share however you like.

3

Share it wherever feels natural

A video description, pinned comment, community post, or link-in-bio — wherever you'd normally point people.

4

Fans add it with one tap

They see exactly which channels are in the pack before adding. Anything they already follow is skipped automatically — no duplicates, nothing overwritten.

Good to know: a pack link carries only channel names and IDs — nothing personal, no saved videos, no account data. It also never overwrites a viewer's existing feed; it only adds to it. Devices hold up to 25 channels, and if a pack would push someone past that, NoSuggest adds as many as it can and reports how many were skipped.
