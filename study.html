The Problem

YouTube Doesn't Know You Have an Exam Tomorrow

The same algorithm that makes YouTube addictive for entertainment doesn't pause just because you opened it to revise.

One lecture ends, autoplay picks the next thing

The moment your teacher's video finishes, autoplay starts something else — rarely another lecture, usually whatever keeps you watching longest.

Shorts are one tap from any lecture

Mid-revision, the Shorts tab is sitting right there. A five-minute break has no natural stopping point and quietly becomes an hour.

Recommendations don't care about your syllabus

YouTube's homepage exists to maximize watch time, not to help you finish today's chapter before tomorrow's test.

The Fix

Only the Lecture Channels You Actually Follow

NoSuggest removes everything YouTube adds to keep you scrolling. What's left is your coaching channels and subject teachers — nothing else.

Zero recommendations, ever

No homepage feed, no sidebar suggestions, no "Up Next." When a lecture ends, nothing plays after it unless you choose it.

No Shorts, anywhere

The Shorts feed doesn't exist inside NoSuggest. There's no rapid-scroll format to fall into between chapters.

Only the channels you add

Add every coaching channel, subject teacher, and PYQ walkthrough you follow. That's the entire feed — nothing else ever surfaces.

Works on any device, no account

Phone, tablet, laptop — NoSuggest runs in the browser or installs like an app. Nothing to sign up for, nothing to download from a store.

For Exam Season

Lock Yourself Into Study Mode

Kids Mode isn't just for parents. Set a PIN, hand it to someone else, and you physically can't add new channels or search for anything outside your list until you get it back.

Set a 6-digit PIN in Kids Mode

This hides the Search tab and the Add Channel screen. Only the channels already on your list are watchable until the PIN is entered.

Give the PIN to someone else

A parent, sibling, or study partner holds it, so you can't turn it off mid-session — even if a part of you wants to.

Worth knowing: this is entirely optional. Most students find that removing recommendations and Shorts alone makes a real difference — Kids Mode is there for exam months when you want a harder line.
