The Problem

YouTube Is Built to Keep You Watching, Not Get You Back to Work

The recommendation engine that makes YouTube great for entertainment is the same one that turns a five-minute lookup into a lost hour.

One video becomes an autoplay chain

You finish the tutorial you actually needed. Autoplay starts the next one before you've even reached for the mouse — and it's rarely related to what you came for.

Shorts hijack a five-minute break

A quick stretch-break scroll through Shorts has no natural stopping point. The format is built to remove the moment you'd normally decide to stop.

Recommendations optimize for watch time, not your goals

YouTube's homepage and sidebar exist to maximize how long you stay on the site. They have no idea you opened the tab to solve one specific problem.

The Fix

A YouTube That Only Shows What You Asked For

NoSuggest strips out everything YouTube adds to keep you scrolling. What's left is just the channels you chose, in the order they posted.

Zero recommendations, ever

No homepage feed, no sidebar suggestions, no "Up Next." When a video ends, nothing plays after it — the decision to keep watching stays yours.

No Shorts, anywhere

The Shorts feed doesn't exist inside NoSuggest. There's no rapid-scroll format to fall into during a short break between meetings.

Only the channels you add

Add the tutorial channels, conference talks, and creators relevant to your work. That's the entire feed — nothing else ever surfaces.

Opens like an app, not a rabbit hole

Install NoSuggest to your dock or home screen as a PWA. It opens instantly to your channels — no browser tabs, no homepage, nothing to scroll past on the way in.
